🐚 About Kanyakumari 🐚

Kanyakumari, also known as Kanniyakumari and formerly called Cape Comorin, is a small yet world-famous town located at the very southern tip of the Indian peninsula in the state of Tamil Nadu. It holds the unique distinction of being the meeting point of three great water bodies — the Arabian Sea, the Bay of Bengal, and the Indian Ocean. Visitors from across the globe travel here to witness the breathtaking sight of the sun rising and setting over the same horizon of ocean waters. The town is home to several iconic landmarks, including the towering Thiruvalluvar Statue, the Vivekananda Rock Memorial, and the ancient Kanyakumari Amman Temple, which give the place deep spiritual and cultural significance. Surrounded by golden beaches, colourful markets, and a rich blend of Tamil traditions, Kanyakumari is not just a geographical landmark but a place brimming with history, natural beauty, and spiritual energy, making it one of the most cherished destinations in all of India.
